# My Mission Reflection

Completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission gave me a more practical understanding of cloud computing, moving beyond textbook concepts and into actual service comparison and decision-making. Among the three platforms I explored AWS, Azure, and GCP - AWS stood out to me the most, largely because of its vast array of services and mature ecosystem. However, as I researched each provider, I noticed that they share a common core: all three offer virtual machines, storage solutions, databases, networking, and identity management. Despite their different terminologies, many of their services address similar use cases, which made it easier to draw meaningful comparisons.

One important realization I gained is that choosing a cloud platform is never just about popularity or brand recognition. Business requirements heavily influence the decision factors such as existing infrastructure, budget, application type, user base, security policies, and future scalability all come into play. For instance, when I acted as a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I recommended Azure for the university because it already relied on Microsoft technologies, making integration smoother. On the other hand, I proposed Google Cloud for the AI research company due to its superior support for machine learning and data analytics. These cases taught me that a good consultant must first understand the client's unique context before offering any solution.

This mission also sharpened my ability to compare cloud services and justify my recommendations with clarity and confidence. I learned that no single platform is universally best, it all depends on the specific needs of the organization. Additionally, I’m glad to say that my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has grown substantially. I added research notes, comparison tables, client proposals, a decision matrix, Linux server configurations, screenshots, and this reflection. Working with Markdown and managing my files through GitHub also became more intuitive over time.

Overall, this mission deepened my understanding of real-world cloud adoption and gave me greater confidence in making informed, client-centered cloud decisions, a skill I find essential as I prepare to enter the IT industry.
