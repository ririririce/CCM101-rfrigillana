# My Mission Reflection

Completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission helped me understand cloud computing in a more practical way. Among the three platforms, **AWS impressed me the most** because of the wide range of services it offers. While researching AWS, Azure, and GCP, I noticed that they have many similarities. All three provide services for virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and other cloud computing needs. Even though they use different names for their services, many of them are designed to solve similar problems. I also learned that choosing a cloud platform is not simply about picking the most popular one. **Business requirements have a big effect on the decision**, such as the company's budget, existing technologies, type of application, number of users, security needs, and future growth. For example, I recommended Azure for the university because it already uses Microsoft technologies, while I recommended Google Cloud for the AI research company because of its strong support for AI and Machine Learning. Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant also taught me that I need to understand the client's situation first before making a recommendation. It made me realize that there is no single cloud platform that is the best for everyone. I also became more familiar with comparing services and explaining why a specific platform would be useful for a particular client. Lastly, I think my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved a lot** after completing this mission. I added research files, comparison tables, client recommendations, a decision matrix, Linux server information, screenshots, and this reflection. I also gained more experience organizing my work using Markdown and managing my files through GitHub. Overall, this mission gave me a better understanding of how cloud platforms are used in real situations and helped me become more confident in making cloud-related decisions.
